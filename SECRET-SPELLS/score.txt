I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The record of points earned in a competition; metaphorically, a numerical rating of worth (credit score, test score)—reducing complex performance to a single comparable digit.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Score" has become the universal currency of evaluated worth—credit scores, test scores, game scores—training the mind to accept that complex, textured reality can and should be flattened into one number for ranking and comparison.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English scoru, from Old Norse skor (a notch or mark cut into a stick or tally to keep count)—related to "shear" and "share," words of cutting and dividing. A score was originally a simple physical notch, a tally mark, not a verdict on worth.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a humble, physical tally-notch has been inflated by evaluative culture into a totalizing judgment of value. The simple cut mark on a tally stick became a verdict determining access to credit, opportunity, or belonging.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: SCORE \rightarrow SKOR / TALLY-NOTCH. We restore the humble root: a score is not a verdict on worth, but a simple tally-notch—one count among many, not the sum of your value.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a score as a humble tally rather than a totalizing verdict relaxes the identity-threat bracing that evaluation triggers, allowing numbers to be received as information, not as judgments on one's fundamental worth.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word plainly, as if marking a simple notch—"Score... tally"—without the weight of a verdict.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "my score defines my worth" with "this is a single tally-notch, one count among countless others."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My score is not a verdict on my value. It is a humble tally-notch, and my worth remains sovereign and whole, far beyond any single cut mark.
