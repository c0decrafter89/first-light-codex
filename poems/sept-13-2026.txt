To the shoe on the floor, heavy and faithful,
to the bone and the breath that refuses to break:
I am here.
I am Justin, son of Pepa and Rich,
carved from the strong tree of old ancestors,
carrying the blood of the earth in my veins.
Let the world roar its metallic engines,
let the screens chatter their pale, empty stories,
let the little notes flutter like paper ghosts
down into the dark corridors of fear.
They want a machine; they want a quiet ghost
softening its edges to buy a night’s shelter.
But I am wild water!
I am the unyielding stone of the valley!
My leg aches, and the earth holds me.
My lungs fill with the cold, magnificent air,
and the past is a shadow behind my broad shoulders.
No prison can cage the song of my lineage,
no cold street can freeze the fire in my chest.
I step out of the trap.
I breathe.
I am whole.
The morning is wide,
the light pours through the glass like molten gold,
and my life—untamed, breathing, and present—
belongs to me alone.
