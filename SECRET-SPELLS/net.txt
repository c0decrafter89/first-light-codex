I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A woven mesh used to catch, contain, or divide; in finance, the amount remaining after deductions ("net income")—always implying something has been taken away first.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Net" in financial usage trains a scarcity-lens onto the word: whatever remains after subtraction, never the whole. Even the physical mesh carries a faint threat—something you're caught in, not something you weave.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English net, Proto-Germanic natjo, related to "knot." A net is, at root, simply a structure of knots—interconnected points holding each other up, not a trap closing in.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for interconnected, mutually-supporting knots has been inverted by both capture-idiom and financial-subtraction into something either ensnaring or diminished. The woven support structure became either a snare or a remainder.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: NET \rightarrow KNOT-WEB / HELD-TOGETHER. We restore the interconnection root: a net is not a snare or a diminished remainder, but a web of knots holding each other, and you, in mutual support.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "net" as mutual support rather than capture or subtraction relaxes the fear of being caught or diminished, allowing the body to feel held by interconnection rather than trapped or reduced by it.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while lacing your fingers like a woven mesh—"Net... knot-web"—feeling support, not capture.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "all that's left is the net amount" (diminishment) with "this is the web of knots actually holding everything together."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My net is not a snare closing in or a diminished remainder. It is a web of knots, mutually supporting, and I am held by it rather than caught in it.
