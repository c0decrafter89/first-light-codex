I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To bring a vehicle to a stop and leave it stationary; also, a protected area of land for recreation—two senses united by the image of a bounded, enclosed space.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Parking" (a vehicle) carries idiomatic weight as an interruption to be resolved quickly ("find parking," the stress of parallel parking)—overshadowing the word's other sense, a park, which names a place of restorative, protected rest.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old French parc (an enclosed hunting ground), ultimately Germanic in origin—a park was originally an enclosed, protected space for game to roam safely. To "park" a vehicle borrows this same image: bringing something into a protected, enclosed space of rest.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for protected, restorative enclosure has been narrowed in vehicular use into a stressful logistical task. The game's safe sanctuary became the driver's parking-anxiety.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: PARK \rightarrow PARC / PROTECTED-SANCTUARY. We restore the sanctuary root: to park is not merely a stressful logistical task, but to bring something into a protected sanctuary—the same safe enclosure a park has always offered.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "parking" as entering protected sanctuary rather than stressful logistics relaxes the anxious bracing around parking-tasks, allowing the act of stopping and resting to be felt as arrival into safety, not a hurdle to clear.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling settled, protected stillness—"Park... parc, protected-sanctuary"—arrival, not stress.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "parking is such a stressful hassle" with "I am bringing this into a protected sanctuary, the same safety a park has always offered."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My parking is not merely a logistical stressor. It is entry into parc, protected sanctuary, and I am sovereign in claiming that safe stillness whenever I arrive.
