I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To leap over an obstacle using hands or a pole; also, a secure, locked chamber for storing valuables—two senses rarely connected, one about soaring motion, the other about sealed containment.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The locked-chamber sense dominates cultural association, training "vault" to feel primarily like sealed, guarded stillness—a strange shadow over the athletic sense, which is actually about soaring release, not containment.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: Both senses trace to Latin volvere (to roll, to turn), by way of Old French voute (an arched, rolled structure) and volter (to leap, to turn in the air). The arched ceiling and the athletic leap share the same root image: a rolling, curved arc through space.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A shared root describing a rolling, arcing curve has split into two divergent cultural associations—one of sealed, guarded stillness (the bank vault), one of soaring motion (the athletic vault)—with the former's heaviness unconsciously shadowing the latter's lightness.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: VAULT \rightarrow VOLVERE / ARCING-ROLL. We restore the shared curve: a vault, whether athletic or architectural, is an arcing roll through space—soaring curvature, not sealed heaviness.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing the vault as an arcing roll rather than a sealed, heavy container invites the body into the full curved trajectory of the leap—hips, spine, and arms following one continuous rolling arc rather than a stiff, braced launch.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while tracing an arc with your hand—"Vault... arcing-roll"—feeling the curve, not a locked box.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace any unconscious heaviness at "vault" with "this is simply an arcing roll through space."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My vault is not a sealed, heavy container. It is an arcing roll through open space, soaring and curved, sovereignly my own.
