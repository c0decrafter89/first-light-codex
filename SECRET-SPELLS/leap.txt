I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A powerful jump, often covering significant distance; metaphorically, a risky, uncertain decision ("a leap of faith")—framing bold action primarily through the lens of its danger.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Leap" in idiom is almost always tied to risk and the unknown—"leap of faith" implies you might fall. This trains hesitation around decisive, confident motion, treating boldness as inherently reckless rather than skilled.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English hleapan (to run, to jump, to leap), related to "lope" (to run with long, easy strides). A leap was originally simply an extension of running—a long, confident stride taken to its natural extreme, not a reckless gamble.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a confident, extended stride has been inverted by risk-idiom into a synonym for uncertain gambling. The skilled runner's natural extension became the anxious gambler's blind bet.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: LEAP \rightarrow HLEAPAN / LONG-STRIDE. We restore the running root: a leap is not a blind gamble, but a long, confident stride—the natural extension of motion already underway.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a leap as an extended stride rather than a reckless gamble allows the legs and core to engage with the same confident readiness as running, rather than the frozen hesitation that "risky leap" framing can trigger.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word mid-stride, as if extending a run—"Leap... long-stride"—feeling continuity with motion already in progress.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "this is a risky leap of faith" with "this is simply my long, confident stride, extended further."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My leap is not a reckless gamble into the unknown. It is my own long, confident stride, sovereignly extended exactly as far as I choose.
