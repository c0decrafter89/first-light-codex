I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To rotate or change direction; also, one's allotted opportunity in sequence ("wait your turn")—a word of both motion and queued patience.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Turn" in queue-culture trains anxious waiting—your turn is scarce, allotted, and must be claimed before it passes, collapsing the fluid, circular motion of the word into a scarce, linear ration.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin tornare (to turn on a lathe), from Greek tornos (a tool for drawing circles, a lathe)—a turn was originally the precise, crafted motion of a tool shaping something round and whole, not a rationed slot in a queue.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for precise, crafted circular motion has been inverted by scarcity-queue culture into an anxiously-awaited, limited allotment. The lathe's smooth revolution became the nervous wait for one's number to be called.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: TURN \rightarrow TORNOS / CRAFTED-REVOLVE. We restore the lathe root: a turn is not a scarce ration to anxiously await, but a crafted revolution—smooth, shaping motion that will come around again.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "turn" as cyclical, crafted motion rather than scarce allotment relaxes the queue-anxiety of waiting, trusting that revolutions, by nature, keep coming around.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while making a slow, smooth circular gesture with your hand—"Turn... tornos"—feeling crafted revolution, not scarcity.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I might miss my turn" with "turns are revolutions, and revolutions keep coming around."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My turn is not a scarce ration I must anxiously defend. It is a crafted revolution, and like all true revolutions, it will come around again.
