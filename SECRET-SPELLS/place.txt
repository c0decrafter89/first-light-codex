I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A particular location or position; also, one's social rank or station ("know your place")—often weaponized to enforce hierarchical confinement.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Place" in hierarchical idiom becomes a cage—"knowing your place" means accepting an assigned, limited position rather than freely occupying open, unbounded location. The word trains confinement where it once named simple position.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin platea (a broad street or open square), from Greek plateia (wide way)—the same root as "plaza" and "plateau." A place was originally a wide, open square, not a narrow, assigned confinement.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for broad, open public space has been inverted by hierarchical idiom into a narrow, assigned rank to be obediently accepted. The wide plaza became a fenced station.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: PLACE \rightarrow PLATEA / WIDE-SQUARE. We restore the open-space root: your place is not a confined, assigned rank, but a wide, open plaza—room enough to move freely within it.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "place" as open plaza rather than hierarchical confinement relaxes the shrinking, obedient bracing that "know your place" idiom conditions, inviting the body into felt spaciousness rather than assigned smallness.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while opening your arms into a wide stance—"Place... platea, wide-square"—claiming space, not confinement.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I need to know my place" (hierarchy-frame) with "my place is a wide, open plaza, with room enough to move."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My place is not an assigned, narrow rank. It is a wide, open plaza, and I am sovereign to move freely across its full breadth.
