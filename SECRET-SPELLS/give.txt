I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To transfer possession of something to another, freely or as part of exchange—in scarcity-conscious culture, often tracked as a depletion of one's own supply.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Give" is treated with accountant's wariness—every giving a subtraction, tallied against one's own finite reserves, training reluctance and quiet resentment around generosity rather than recognizing giving as a form of circulation.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English giefan, Proto-Indo-European ghabh- (to give or to receive)—remarkably, the root itself holds both directions at once, suggesting giving and receiving were never truly separate acts, but two faces of the same flow.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A root that held giving and receiving as one unified flow has been split by scarcity-economics into a one-way depletion. The circulating exchange became a strict subtraction from a hoarded account.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: GIVE \rightarrow GHABH / FLOW-BOTH-WAYS. We restore the dual-direction root: to give is not pure depletion, but one half of a continuous flow that circulates back, in its own time and form.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing giving as circulation rather than pure depletion relaxes the hoarding-anxiety that scarcity-tracking trains, allowing generosity to move through the body as exchange, not loss.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word with an open, outward gesture of the hand that then circles back toward the chest—"Give... ghabh, flow"—feeling circulation, not depletion.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "giving this depletes my limited supply" with "giving is one half of a continuous flow that circulates back to me."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My giving is not a one-way drain on a finite account. It is half of an ancient, unified flow, ghabh, and I remain sovereign within its circulation.
