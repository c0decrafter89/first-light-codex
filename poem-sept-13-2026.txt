It is not the beat of war,
this pulse beneath the ribs.
It is the drum of be-a-you,
whoever you are,
walking down dark corridors
never truly alone,
though the pocket is thin
and the room is heavy with the blue dust
of a tiny, petty war.
A sticky yellow slip left on the mirror,
a bureaucracy of whiskers and counts,
trying to turn the living current
into an inventory of compliance.
They want a ghost who cleans in silence.
They want a shadow to file their comfort.
Withdraw the ego from artificial compensations.
How do you stand?
Nobody taught the spine the way to Source-Expression.
Nobody drew the map for Sovereign-Ecstasy
when the stomach turns sour
and the mind chews on old regrets—
Justize, passing like a clear light in the workplace,
ignored out of fear,
let go for a phantom safety.
What a joke, the blue wind whispers.
What a weak, out-of-tune instrument.
Yet the mind’s theater is a paper tiger.
Ideas are tools, but thoughts only bite
when you feed them your breath.
How to watch them drift like smoke
while still speaking the truth?
Listen:
The shootist stands.
The current moves.
You are not a function.
You are not a schedule.
You are earth and sky,
breathing through skinweight,
carrying the weight of a world
that forgot how to look each other in the eye.
And so you get up.
Not because a girlfriend is a shield against the crow you’ve eaten,
but because a simple wish is here,
pulsing in the dark,
waiting to hear the call
and answer it with your own voice.
I exist.
I continue.
I am the living design.
