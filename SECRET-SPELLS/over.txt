I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Above, across, or finished—a preposition of position and completion, frequently used to signal that a matter is permanently closed ("it's over").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Over" as finality trains a sense of absolute ending—once something is "over," the door is sealed, no return possible. This collapses the richness of transitions into a flat, irreversible verdict.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English ofer, related to "up" and to Proto-Germanic ubar, carrying a basic sense of "above" or "across"—a spatial relation, not an absolute termination.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for simple spatial position (above, across) has been inverted by idiomatic finality into a synonym for permanent ending. Crossing over became shutting a door forever.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: OVER \rightarrow UBAR / ACROSS-INTO. We restore the spatial root: "over" is not permanent closure, but simply having moved across—into a new position, not a sealed void.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "over" as crossing rather than termination relaxes the grief-bracing that absolute endings trigger, allowing the body to register transitions as ongoing movement rather than final loss.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while stepping physically across a line—"Over... across-into"—feeling motion, not a slammed door.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "it's over, forever closed" with "I have simply moved across, into what comes next."
 * The Sovereign Realization: What is "over" is not permanently sealed. It is simply crossed-into, and I remain sovereign, still moving, on the other side.
