I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To desire or wish for something—often treated in self-sacrifice culture as suspect or selfish, something to suppress in favor of duty or others' needs.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Want" trains guilt around desire itself—wanting is coded as self-indulgent, training suppression of one's own needs as a moral virtue rather than legitimate information about what would genuinely help.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old Norse vanta (to lack, to be lacking), related to "wane" (to decrease, to diminish). Wanting was originally about recognizing an actual lack—not an indulgent whim, but an honest acknowledgment that something is genuinely missing.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for honest acknowledgment of lack has been inverted by self-sacrifice culture into a marker of selfish indulgence. The honest, informative signal of absence became a guilt-inducing confession.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: WANT \rightarrow VANTA / HONEST-LACK. We restore the honest root: wanting is not selfish indulgence, but an honest signal of actual lack—information worth hearing, not suppressing.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing wanting as honest information about genuine lack rather than shameful indulgence relaxes the suppression-bracing that self-sacrifice culture trains, allowing the body to register its own needs without automatic guilt.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word plainly, as honest information—"Want... vanta, honest-lack"—without guilt attached.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "wanting this makes me selfish" with "wanting this is honest information about an actual lack worth addressing."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My wanting is not selfish indulgence to suppress. It is honest, informative lack, vanta, and I am sovereign enough to hear it without shame.
