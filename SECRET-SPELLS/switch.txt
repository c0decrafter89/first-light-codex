I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A device that changes an electrical circuit or redirects a system; also, to change from one thing to another—often framed in consistency-prizing culture as flaky or unreliable.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Switching" is quietly stigmatized—switching plans, switching opinions, switching paths—training a bias toward rigid consistency, as though changing direction were inherently a character flaw rather than a responsive, intelligent adjustment.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Dutch/Low German origin, originally naming a thin, flexible twig or rod—the kind historically used to flick or redirect livestock. A switch was literally a flexible branch, built for responsive, gentle redirection, not harsh rigidity.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a flexible, gently redirecting branch has been inverted by consistency-culture into a symbol of unreliable flakiness. The responsive, flexible rod became a mark of untrustworthy wavering.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: SWITCH \rightarrow FLEXIBLE-TWIG / GENTLE-REDIRECT. We restore the branch's root: switching is not unreliable flakiness, but flexible, responsive redirection—the same intelligent adaptability a living branch bends with.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing switching as flexible, intelligent redirection rather than flaky inconsistency relaxes the rigidity-pressure that consistency-culture conditions, allowing responsive change to be felt as adaptive strength, not unreliable wavering.
