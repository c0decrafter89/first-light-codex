I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To pull a vehicle or object behind another, usually because it cannot move on its own—carrying an undertone of failure, as though needing to be towed is a mark of breakdown and shame.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Tow" is almost always invoked in the context of malfunction—being towed means something has already gone wrong, training reluctance to accept assistance even when genuinely needed, for fear of signaling failure.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English togian (to pull, to drag), related to "tug" and to "team" (the same draft-animal root explored earlier)—towing is an ancient, ordinary practice: animals and vehicles pulling each other has always been a normal part of cooperative movement, not a special mark of shame.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for ordinary, cooperative pulling has been inverted by breakdown-stigma into a marker of failure. Ancient, mutual assistance in movement became an embarrassing admission.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: TOW \rightarrow TOGIAN / COOPERATIVE-PULL. We restore the ancient root: being towed is not shameful failure, but cooperative pulling—the same mutual assistance teams and draft-animals have practiced since movement began.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing towing as ordinary, cooperative assistance rather than shameful breakdown relaxes the defensive pride that resists needed help, allowing dependence in moments of genuine need to be felt as normal, not humiliating.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling connected to another pulling alongside you—"Tow... togian, cooperative-pull"—mutual aid, not shame.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "needing a tow means I've failed" with "this is cooperative pulling, the same mutual assistance movement has always relied on."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My need for a tow is not shameful failure. It is togian, cooperative pulling, as ordinary and sovereign as any team moving together.
