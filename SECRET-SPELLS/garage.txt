I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A structure for storing a vehicle, or a facility for repairs—often associated in domestic idiom with clutter, the dumping ground for things that don't fit elsewhere in the house.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Garage" as the household's catch-all clutter zone trains an association between the word and chaotic, neglected accumulation, overshadowing its actual original purpose as a dedicated, protective shelter.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From French garage, from garer (to shelter, to dock a ship, to protect), related to "guard" and "ward." A garage was originally, and fundamentally, a place of deliberate sheltering and protection—the same root family as safeguarding.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for deliberate, protective sheltering has been inverted by domestic-clutter culture into a chaotic storage dump. The guarded, protective shelter became the neglected junk-room.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: GARAGE \rightarrow GARER / PROTECTIVE-SHELTER. We restore the sheltering root: a garage is not a chaotic dump, but a protective shelter—a guarded space, same root as "guard," deliberately sheltering what matters.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing the garage as deliberate, protective shelter rather than clutter-chaos invites a felt sense of order and care toward the space, settling the body into the same guarding intention the word has always carried.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while picturing a guarded, sheltered space—"Garage... garer, protective-shelter"—order, not chaos.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "my garage is just a chaotic junk-room" with "my garage is a protective shelter, guarding what I choose to keep there."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My garage is not a neglected dump. It is garer, a protective shelter, and I am sovereign in restoring its original, guarding purpose.
