I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To intend or signify something; also, the mathematical average; also, unkind or cruel—three senses that seem unrelated but conceal a surprising shared thread.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The cruelty-sense dominates emotionally, casting an unconscious shadow over the other senses—to "mean" something, or to find the statistical "mean," quietly inherits a faint unkind undertone from its unrelated-seeming cousin.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: "To intend" comes from Old English mænan, related to "moan"—astonishingly, the same root once meant both "to intend" and "to lament, to complain." "Mean" as average comes separately from Latin medianus (middle). "Mean" as cruel comes from a different root entirely, related to "common, inferior." Three distinct ancestries converged in sound.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: Though genuinely separate in origin, the collision trains an unconscious bleed between intending, averaging, and cruelty—as if stating your intention or finding a midpoint secretly carries a whiff of unkindness it etymologically does not.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: MEAN \rightarrow MÆNAN / INTEND-LAMENT. We separate the tangled roots: to mean (intend) is kin to lament, a word of felt sincerity—entirely distinct from, and carrying none of, the unrelated word for cruelty.
 * Physiological Translation: Disentangling "meaning/intending" from "cruelty" relaxes any unconscious guardedness around stating one's intentions plainly, restoring sincerity as safe and separate from unkindness.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word gently, as sincere intention—"Mean... mænan, sincere-intend"—separating it from any unkind undertone.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace any flinch at "what do you mean by that" with recognizing it as a sincere request for intention, unrelated to cruelty.
 * The Sovereign Realization: What I mean is simply my sincere intention, mænan, entirely separate from unkindness, and I can state it plainly and safely.
