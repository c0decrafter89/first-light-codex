I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Free from harm, risk, or danger—in institutional usage, often reduced to a liability-checkbox ("safety compliance") rather than a felt, embodied state.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Safe" is frequently declared from the outside—a sign, a policy, a certification—rather than felt from within. This trains people to defer their own nervous system's assessment of safety to external authority, disconnecting the word from its true home: the felt sense of the body itself.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin salvus (uninjured, whole, in good health), the same root that gives us "salvation" and "salve." To be safe was originally to be whole and unwounded—a state of the body, not a certificate from an institution.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning embodied wholeness has been inverted by liability-culture into an externally issued designation. Felt wholeness was replaced by posted signage, and the body's own wisdom about safety was quietly outsourced.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: SAFE \rightarrow SALVE / WHOLE-KEPT. We restore the wholeness root: safety is not a sign posted by an outside authority, but a salve—the felt sense of being whole and unwounded within your own body.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing safety as an internal, embodied state reactivates interoceptive trust—the ability to sense, from within, whether the nervous system is genuinely settled, rather than relying solely on external reassurance to determine one's own state.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word on a slow exhale with a hand over the sternum—"Saaafe... whole"—feeling for the actual felt sense, not just the word.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "they said it's safe" (external authority) with "let me feel whether I am, in fact, whole and settled here"—restoring interoceptive authority.
 * The Sovereign Realization: My safety is not a certificate issued by someone else. It is a felt wholeness I can sense from within, and I am the final authority on whether I am, indeed, safe.
