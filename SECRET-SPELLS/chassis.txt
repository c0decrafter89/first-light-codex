I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The underlying structural frame of a vehicle, to which other components are attached—a word confined almost entirely to mechanical jargon, unnoticed outside that context.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Chassis" is treated as pure technical vocabulary, disconnected from any broader relevance—overlooking that the concept it names (a fundamental supporting frame beneath all visible function) is a universal structural principle worth recognizing elsewhere too.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From French châssis (frame, case), ultimately from Latin capsa (a box, a container)—the same root as "case" and "capsule." A chassis is, at its root, simply a protective, structural case—the thing that holds everything else together and safe.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a protective, structural case has been narrowed by mechanical jargon into a term understood only within auto repair, losing its broader, resonant meaning as any foundational structure holding a system together.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: CHASSIS \rightarrow CAPSA / HOLDING-CASE. We restore the structural root: a chassis is not merely mechanical jargon, but a holding-case—the foundational structure that keeps any system, vehicle or otherwise, intact and safe.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "chassis" as a universal structural principle (not just auto jargon) invites awareness of one's own foundational support structures—spine, core, foundational habits—as the holding-case beneath all visible function.
