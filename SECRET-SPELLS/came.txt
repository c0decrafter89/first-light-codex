I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The past tense of "come"—arrival, now relegated to history, something that has already happened and is therefore closed.
 * The Acoustic Trap: As a past-tense form, "came" trains the mind to file arrivals away as completed, finished events, severing the living arrival from its ongoing resonance in the present moment.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English cuman, Proto-Indo-European gwa- (to go, to come, to step)—the same ancient root family as "go." Arrival and departure were never truly separate motions; both are simply the body stepping through space.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a step through space, equally living in the present as in the past, has been flattened by strict tense-grammar into something sealed in history. The ongoing step was locked behind a grammatical door marked "finished."
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: CAME \rightarrow GWA / STILL-STEPPING. We restore the ongoing root: what "came" is not sealed in dead history—it is a step that is still part of the same unbroken motion carrying you forward now.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a past arrival as part of continuous motion rather than a sealed, finished event allows old arrivals (relationships, changes, griefs) to be felt as still-resonating current, not inert history.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while taking an actual step forward—"Came... gwa, still-stepping"—feeling continuity, not closure.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "that already came and went, it's over" with "that step is part of the same unbroken motion still carrying me."
 * The Sovereign Realization: What came to me is not sealed behind a closed door of history. It is one living step in an unbroken motion, and I remain sovereign within its continuing current.
