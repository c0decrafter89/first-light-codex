I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A marked playing area for sports like basketball or tennis; also, a legal tribunal, or the formal residence and retinue of a monarch.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The legal and royal senses dominate the word's gravity, lending even the sports court an unconscious undertone of judgment and hierarchy—every game played "on the court" borrows a faint shadow of the courtroom's verdict-power.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old French cort, from Latin cohors/cohortis (an enclosed yard, a retinue, a group gathered together)—the same root as "cohort." A court was originally simply an enclosed gathering space, not a seat of judgment.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning "enclosed gathering space" has been narrowed by legal and royal institutions into a site of hierarchical verdict and power. The shared yard became the judgment chamber.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: COURT \rightarrow COHORS / GATHERED-YARD. We restore the gathering root: a court is not inherently a site of judgment, but an enclosed yard where a cohort gathers—shared space for collective activity, not verdict.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing the court as gathered-space rather than judgment-chamber relaxes the performance-anxiety ("being judged on the court") into the felt ease of simply being present among one's cohort.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while picturing a gathered group of peers, not a judge's bench—"Court... cohort"—restoring companionship over verdict.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I'm being judged on this court" with "I am simply gathered here with my cohort, in shared space."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My court is not a judgment chamber deciding my worth. It is a gathered yard, shared with my cohort, for collective, sovereign activity.
