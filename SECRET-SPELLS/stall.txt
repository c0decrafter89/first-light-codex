I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: An engine unexpectedly ceasing to run; metaphorically, to delay or avoid progress—often coded as embarrassing failure or deliberate, cowardly avoidance.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Stalling" trains shame around unplanned pauses—whether an engine or a life plan, stalling is coded as a humiliating malfunction, obscuring that the underlying image (a standing place) is actually neutral and stable.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English steall (a standing place, a position, a stable), related to "stand" and "stable." A stall was originally simply a place where something stands—a stable, settled position, not a humiliating malfunction.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a stable, settled standing-place has been inverted by progress-obsessed culture into a marker of embarrassing failure or cowardly avoidance. Simply standing still became a shameful breakdown.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: STALL \rightarrow STEALL / STABLE-STANDING. We restore the stable root: a stall is not humiliating failure, but a standing-place—a stable pause, like a horse resting in its own steall, not a breakdown to be ashamed of.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a stall as a stable, settled standing-place rather than shameful failure relaxes the embarrassment-bracing around unplanned pauses, allowing unexpected stillness to be met with the same ease as a horse resting in its stable.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while settling into a stable, grounded stance—"Stall... steall, stable-standing"—ease, not shame.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I stalled, how embarrassing, I failed" with "I have simply arrived at a stable standing-place, like a horse resting in its stall."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My stall is not a humiliating breakdown. It is steall, a stable standing-place, and I remain sovereign, resting there until I am ready to move on.
