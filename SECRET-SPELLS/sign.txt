I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A marker conveying information or instruction; also, to formally commit via signature—a word spanning both passive observation and binding legal commitment.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Sign" as legal commitment ("sign here") carries institutional weight and risk-anxiety, training wariness around the broader word, as though every sign encountered might secretly demand binding obligation.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin signum (a mark, a token, a symbol)—the same root as "signal," "design," and "significant." A sign was originally simply a meaningful mark, pointing toward something worth noticing—not inherently a binding legal document.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a meaningful, informative mark has been narrowed by legal-contract anxiety into near-exclusive association with binding, risky commitment. Simple, helpful pointing became institutional liability.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: SIGN \rightarrow SIGNUM / MEANINGFUL-MARK. We restore the broader root: a sign is simply a meaningful mark—most signs merely inform or point, and need carry no automatic weight of binding risk.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "sign" as broadly meaningful-mark rather than automatically risky-commitment relaxes the contractual anxiety that legal-signing culture can project onto the word in general, restoring ease when encountering ordinary, informative signs.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word plainly, as a simple pointer—"Sign... signum, meaningful-mark"—not automatic liability.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace automatic wariness at "sign" with "most signs are simply meaningful marks, pointing toward something worth noticing."
 * The Sovereign Realization: A sign is not automatically a binding risk. It is signum, a meaningful mark, and I remain sovereign in discerning which signs genuinely call for commitment and which simply inform.
