I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To use the mind to form ideas, consider, or reason—in Cartesian, hyper-cognitive culture, elevated as the primary (sometimes only) legitimate mode of knowing, above feeling or sensing.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Think" has been enthroned above all other ways of knowing—"I think, therefore I am" trains the mind to treat cognition as the sole proof of existence, demoting felt, embodied, intuitive knowing as lesser.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English þencan, related to þoht ("thought," already explored), ultimately connected through Proto-Germanic roots to a sense of "feeling" (teng-)—thinking and feeling were never truly separate activities at the word's origin.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word originally entangled with feeling has been inverted by Cartesian, hyper-rational culture into a cognition severed from the body. The felt-thought became pure, disembodied mentation.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: THINK \rightarrow ÞENCAN / FELT-COGNITION. We restore the entangled root: to think is not disembodied cognition alone, but felt-cognition—mind and body knowing together, as they were always meant to.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing thinking as inherently felt rather than purely abstract invites the body back into cognitive process, settling the nervous system into integrated, whole-being knowing rather than isolated headspace.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while placing a hand on your belly, not just your head—"Think... þencan, felt-cognition"—reuniting mind and body.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I need to think my way through this" (head-only) with "I am thinking with my whole felt body, not my mind alone."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My thinking is not disembodied mentation severed from feeling. It is felt-cognition, þencan, my whole sovereign being knowing together.
