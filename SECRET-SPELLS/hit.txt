I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To strike with force; metaphorically, a sudden affecting realization ("it hit me") or a popular success ("a hit song").
 * The Acoustic Trap: The violent-strike sense dominates, casting a shadow over the word's gentler meanings—"hit" trains an association between contact and harm, even when the contact in question (a realization, a success, a well-placed ball) carries no violence at all.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old Norse hitta (to meet with, to come upon, to find)—the original sense was encounter, not aggression. To "hit" something once simply meant you found it, met it, arrived at it.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning "to meet, to find" has been inverted by combat-and-violence framing into near-exclusive association with striking force. Simple arrival became assault.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: HIT \rightarrow HITTA / MEET-FIND. We restore the encounter root: a hit is not inherently violent—it is an accurate meeting, a successful finding of the mark.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a hit as accurate encounter rather than inherent violence allows athletic striking to be felt as precise connection rather than aggression, settling the nervous system into focused accuracy instead of combative tension.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word crisply but without clenched aggression—"Hit... meet"—as if naming an accurate arrival.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I have to hit it hard" (violence-frame) with "I simply need to meet it accurately."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My hit is not an act of violence. It is an accurate meeting, a precise finding of the mark—skill, not aggression.
