I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The central rod around which a wheel rotates—mechanically essential yet almost entirely invisible, overshadowed by the wheel's more visible motion.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Axle" is treated as a mere background part, unworthy of attention compared to the dramatic rolling of the wheel it supports—training a habit of overlooking the still, central support that makes visible motion possible at all.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old Norse öxull, related to Latin axis—the same ancient root behind "axis," the fundamental line around which anything turns. An axle is, at its deepest root, simply an axis given physical form.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the fundamental axis—the still point around which all turning becomes possible—has been reduced by mechanical anonymity into an overlooked, minor component beneath the more dramatic wheel. The still center was eclipsed by the visible motion it enables.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: AXLE \rightarrow AXIS / STILL-CENTER. We restore the axis root: an axle is not a minor, overlooked part, but the still center—the fundamental point of stillness that makes all visible turning possible.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing the axle as the still, central axis rather than a minor component invites awareness of one's own still center (spine, breath, inner stillness) as the enabling point around which all outward motion turns.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling your own central stillness, even as you move—"Axle... axis, still-center"—honoring the quiet core.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "the axle is just a minor part" with "this is the still center, the axis making all visible motion possible."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My axle is not an overlooked, minor component. It is axis, the still center, and I am sovereign in honoring the quiet point around which all my motion turns.
