I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A comparative quantifier denoting a greater quantity, degree, amount, or additional supply of something, functioning as the foundational engine of consumerism, accumulation, and endless industrial growth.
 * The Acoustic Trap: More acts as the ultimate loop of lack. By its very linguistic structure, uttering the word more asserts that the present moment is inherently deficient. It triggers a chronic neurological phantom-itch, binding dopamine circuitry to a horizon line that recedes forever. It is a linguistic ghost-hunger that commands the body to brace, reach, and consume, converting a state of natural biological sufficiency into an eternal, scarcity-driven race against an imaginary finish line.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: Stemming from the Proto-Germanic maiz (greater) and Old English māra, the sonic arc of more echoes mors (death) and morass (a sinking bog). Acoustically, it launches with a closed, humming intake of breath (m-)—like a trapped vibration—and grinds out on a hard, driving -or that demands an endless extension, terminating in a soft, exhausted expiration (-e). It has been hijacked from a simple metric of natural abundance and weaponized by economic architectures to mean "never enough."
 * The Inversion of Meaning: In natural ecosystems, expansion is cyclical, self-limiting, and balanced by decay and rest; a cell or an organism reaches its native form and thrives in proportion. The industrial translation of more inverts this by turning growth into a moral imperative and an infinite moral debt. It divorces human desire from somatic satiety, teaching the nervous system that stillness equals stagnation and that value only exists in an ongoing accumulation of external mass.
