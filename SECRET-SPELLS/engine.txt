I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A machine converting energy into mechanical motion—in industrial-mechanistic culture, the central metaphor for the body itself, reduced to a soulless combustion unit to be fueled and maintained.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Engine" as body-metaphor ("the human engine," "running on empty") trains a cold, mechanistic self-relationship—just parts converting fuel to output, stripped of any inherent ingenuity or aliveness.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin ingenium (innate talent, cleverness, inborn genius)—the same root as "ingenious" and "genius." An engine was never meant to be soulless; its very name means inborn brilliance, inventive spirit made mechanical.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning innate genius and inventive spirit has been inverted by industrial-mechanistic culture into the paradigm of soulless, replaceable machinery. Inborn brilliance became cold combustion.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: ENGINE \rightarrow INGENIUM / INBORN-GENIUS. We restore the ingenious root: an engine is not soulless machinery, but inborn genius given mechanical form—inventive spirit, not dead combustion.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "engine" (including the body's own metaphorical engine) as inborn genius rather than soulless machinery invites the body to be met with the same reverence given to any ingenious, living creation, not mere fuel-to-output hardware.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling your own vitality—"Engine... ingenium, inborn-genius"—claiming ingenuity, not mere mechanism.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "my body is just an engine to fuel" with "my body is ingenium, inborn genius, inventive and alive."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My engine is not soulless machinery. It is ingenium, my own inborn genius, sovereign and inventive, not reducible to mere fuel and output.
