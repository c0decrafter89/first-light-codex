I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A close connection between people; financially, a legal instrument of debt and obligation posted as security.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The financial sense contaminates the relational one—"bond" carries an unconscious undertone of debt, collateral, and enforceable obligation, as though every close connection secretly requires posted security against betrayal, rather than being freely, mutually chosen.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old Norse band (a fastening, a tie), the same ancient root as "bind" and "band." Originally, a bond was simply a tie holding things together—no debt, no collateral, just connection.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning simple, mutual connection has been inverted by financial-legal culture into a security instrument backed by enforceable debt. The freely-tied connection became a collateralized obligation.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: BOND \rightarrow BAND-TIE / FREE-FASTENING. We restore the simple-tie root: a bond between people is not collateral against betrayal, but a free fastening, held together by mutual will, not legal enforcement.
 * Physiological Translation: Releasing the debt-and-collateral undertone from relational bonds relaxes the anxious, transactional vigilance ("what do I owe, what's owed to me") that financial framing smuggles into intimacy, restoring a felt sense of connection freely given and freely received.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while gently pressing your palms together—"Bond... free-tie"—feeling connection rather than contractual weight.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "what does this bond obligate me to" (debt-frame) with "this bond is a freely-chosen tie, renewed by willingness, not enforced by contract."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My bonds are not collateralized debts requiring enforcement. They are freely fastened ties, held together only by ongoing, mutual, sovereign will.
