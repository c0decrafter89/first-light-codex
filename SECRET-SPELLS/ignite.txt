I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To set fire to or cause to burn; metaphorically, to spark intense feeling or action—often spoken of with wariness as something volatile that might spread beyond control.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Ignite" carries an automatic caution-flag in fire-safety-conscious culture, training a wary relationship to one's own capacity for sudden, intense combustion—whether literal flame or metaphorical passion—as inherently hazardous.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin ignis (fire), the same elemental root explored under "ignition." To ignite is simply to bring forth fire—the ancient, foundational act that gave early humans warmth, light, cooked food, and protection, long before it was coded primarily as danger.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the foundational, civilization-enabling gift of fire has been inverted by safety-regulation culture into an automatic warning sign. The gift that built human civilization became primarily a liability to control.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: IGNITE \rightarrow IGNIS / FOUNDATIONAL-FIRE. We restore the elemental root: to ignite is not primarily a hazard, but to bring forth foundational fire—the same gift that built warmth, light, and human flourishing.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing ignition as foundational, generative fire rather than primarily danger invites the body to welcome its own sparks of passion and creative combustion as life-building, not threatening.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling warmth kindle in your chest—"Ignite... ignis, foundational-fire"—welcoming, not bracing.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "this spark is dangerous, I need to contain it" with "this is ignis, foundational fire, the same gift that has always built and warmed."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My ignition is not primarily a hazard to fear. It is ignis, foundational fire, and I am sovereign enough to welcome my own generative sparks.
