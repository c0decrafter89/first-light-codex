I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A brief, subtle flash or glow of light—often used to describe a fleeting spark of emotion or intent (a gleam in the eye).
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Gleam" is almost always framed as momentary and slight, a mere flicker easily dismissed rather than a genuine, trustworthy signal of something real emerging into visibility. The word's smallness trains observers to underestimate what it actually announces.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English glæm (brightness, splendor, radiance), related to "glimmer" and "glow"—all descendants of an ancient root for shining light. Far from a trivial flicker, "gleam" once carried the full weight of splendor and radiance.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word once meaning full radiant splendor has been diminished by casual usage into a passing, easily-dismissed flicker. The full brightness was shrunk into something barely worth noticing.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: GLEAM \rightarrow GLÆM / FULL-RADIANCE. We restore the splendor root: a gleam is not a trivial flicker, but a genuine glimpse of full radiance—splendor announcing itself, however briefly.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a gleam as authentic radiance rather than a dismissible flicker invites full presence and attention when it appears—in another's eyes, in one's own reflection, in a passing moment—rather than glancing past it as insignificant.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word slowly, letting the vowel shine—"Gleeeam... radiance"—as if the sound itself were the light it describes.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "just a gleam, nothing much" with "this gleam is genuine radiance, worth my full attention."
 * The Sovereign Realization: A gleam is not a trivial flicker to dismiss. It is splendor briefly announcing itself, and I am sovereign enough to receive it fully, however brief its visit.
