I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Tied, restricted, or obligated (past tense of "bind"); also, a limit or boundary marking the edge of a territory.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The collision of "tied down" and "boundary line" trains an unconscious equation between restriction and containment—as though every limit is a form of captivity, and every boundary a cage rather than a chosen edge that makes safety and selfhood possible.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: The two senses have distinct roots: "bound" (tied) descends from Old English bindan (to bind), while "bound" (limit) comes from Old French bodne, a boundary marker. They converged in sound but not in origin—one is about connection, the other about definition.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: Because the two words sound identical, culture conflates being tied against one's will with having a defined edge. This inversion makes healthy boundaries feel like captivity, when a boundary was never meant to be a bind—it was meant to be a definition of self.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: BOUND \rightarrow BOD-NE / SELF-DEFINED-EDGE. We separate the tangled roots: my bounds are not chains but bodne—the self-drawn edges that define where I begin and end, chosen rather than imposed.
 * Physiological Translation: Distinguishing chosen boundary from imposed restraint releases the trapped, claustrophobic bracing that "bound" can trigger, replacing it with the grounded, contained ease of a self-defined edge—firm, not confining.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while tracing the outline of your own body with your hands—"Bound... my own edge"—feeling definition, not captivity.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I feel so bound" (captivity-frame) with "I am simply defining my own edge here"—restoring boundary as sovereignty.
 * The Sovereign Realization: What binds me by force is not the same as what defines me by choice. My true bounds are self-drawn edges, and within them I am free, not caged.
