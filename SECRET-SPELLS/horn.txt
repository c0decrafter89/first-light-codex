I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A vehicle's audible warning device; anatomically, a hard protrusion grown by certain animals—used in idiom as a symbol of aggression ("honking," "butting horns," road rage expressed through sound).
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Horn" in traffic context is almost exclusively associated with aggression and alarm—a blast of frustration, training the word toward hostility rather than its original, broader connection to resonant, ceremonial sound (the hunting horn, the musical horn).
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English horn, Proto-Indo-European ker- (horn, head)—the same ancient root behind "cornea," "unicorn," and "cornet" (a musical horn). The root connects animal horns to musical instruments, both resonant, both meant to carry sound and signal across distance with dignity.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word connected to resonant, ceremonial, musical signaling has been narrowed by road-rage association into pure aggressive alarm. The dignified cornet became the frustrated blast.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: HORN \rightarrow CORNET / RESONANT-SIGNAL. We restore the musical root: a horn's sound is not inherently aggressive alarm, but resonant signal—the same dignified carrying-of-sound as a ceremonial cornet.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing the horn as resonant signal rather than aggressive alarm invites a more measured, intentional relationship to sounding it—using it as deliberate communication, not reflexive hostility.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while picturing a musical cornet, not a blast of rage—"Horn... cornet, resonant-signal"—dignity, not hostility.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "my horn is for venting frustration" with "my horn is a resonant signal, used deliberately to communicate."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My horn is not a weapon of aggressive alarm. It is cornet, a resonant signal, and I am sovereign in sounding it with dignified, deliberate intention.
