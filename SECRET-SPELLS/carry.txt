I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To bear, transport, or support the weight of something—often extended metaphorically to emotional or psychological burden ("carrying trauma," "carrying the weight of the world").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Carry" in therapeutic and cultural shorthand has calcified into a synonym for chronic, uncomplaining burden-bearing—the martyred posture of the one who silently hauls what others will not. The word trains the body into a permanent, braced-shoulder stance, as if release were never actually an option.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old French carier, from carre (cart, wagon)—the same root as "car" and "chariot." To "carry" was originally to cart, to move something via a wheeled vessel designed specifically for transport, not to strain under unaided personal weight.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word describing wheeled, mechanically-assisted transport has been inverted by stoic-endurance culture into unaided, martyred bearing—as if every burden must be hauled on bare shoulders rather than moved via some form of supportive vessel or shared load.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: CARRY \rightarrow CART-ED / CHARIOT-BORNE. We restore the wheeled-vessel root: to carry something is to cart it, using support and momentum, not to strain it alone across bare shoulders.
 * Physiological Translation: Reframing "carrying" as cart-assisted transport rather than unaided strain relaxes the chronically braced trapezius and lower back—the body's habitual martyr-posture. The nervous system recognizes that weight can move via supported, shared momentum, not only through grim, solitary endurance.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word with a rolling, wheel-like cadence—"Carrr-y"—imagining wheels turning beneath the weight rather than shoulders straining under it.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I have to carry this alone" with "I can cart this forward with support, momentum, and shared load"—restoring the vessel over the martyr.
 * The Sovereign Realization: I do not have to haul every weight on my bare shoulders. I am allowed a chariot—support, momentum, and shared carrying—for whatever I must move forward.
