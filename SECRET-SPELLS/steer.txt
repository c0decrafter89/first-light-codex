I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To guide the direction of a vehicle or situation—in micromanagement-anxious culture, often implying the need for constant, vigilant correction against any drift.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Steer" trains hyper-vigilance against drift, as though any deviation from an exact line is a crisis requiring immediate correction, rather than a normal, expected part of any journey's actual path.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English stieran (to guide, to direct), related to "stern" and "stir"—steering was always understood as the stern's gentle, continuous adjustment, not a single perfect line held through constant white-knuckled vigilance.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for gentle, continuous adjustment from the stern has been inverted by perfectionist-vigilance culture into a demand for flawless, uninterrupted line-holding. Natural, ongoing correction became an anxious crisis-response.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: STEER \rightarrow STIERAN / GENTLE-STERN-ADJUST. We restore the stern's gentle root: steering is not flawless, vigilant line-holding, but gentle, continuous adjustment—small corrections, naturally and repeatedly made.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing steering as gentle, ongoing adjustment rather than demanded perfection relaxes the hyper-vigilant bracing of micromanagement-anxiety, allowing small corrections to be felt as normal, not crisis-driven.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word with a light, continuous adjusting motion of the hands—"Steer... stieran, gentle-adjust"—not a desperate grip.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "any drift means I'm failing to steer" with "gentle, ongoing adjustment is exactly what steering has always meant."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My steering is not flawless, vigilant line-holding. It is gentle, continuous adjustment, stieran, and sovereign, ordinary correction is already enough.
