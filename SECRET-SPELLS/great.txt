I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Of considerable size, extent, or quality—in achievement culture, a superlative status reserved for the exceptional few, implying everyone else is merely ordinary.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Great" has been elevated into a rare, exclusive status marker ("great men of history"), training the sense that greatness is a scarce designation bestowed on a select few, rather than a quality available in ordinary, embodied experience.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English great (thick, massive, stout, coarse)—the oldest sense was purely physical: thickness, bulk, substantiality. It originally described a body's mass, not an exceptional rank among the few.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for simple physical thickness and substance has been inflated by achievement-culture into a rare, exclusive marker of exceptional status. Ordinary bodily substance became scarce historical grandeur.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: GREAT \rightarrow GREAT / THICK-SUBSTANTIAL. We restore the physical root: great is not a rare, exclusive status, but simple substantiality—thickness, fullness, presence, available to any body in any moment.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "great" as physical substantiality rather than exclusive rare status invites a felt sense of one's own bodily thickness and presence as already, simply great, without needing historical exceptionalism to qualify.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling the actual mass and thickness of your own body—"Great... thick-substantial"—claiming presence, not rare exceptionalism.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I'm not great, I'm not exceptional" with "I am substantial, thick with presence, and that is already great in the word's oldest sense."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My greatness is not a rare designation reserved for the exceptional few. It is my own substantial, thick, embodied presence, sovereignly great in the word's oldest, truest sense.
