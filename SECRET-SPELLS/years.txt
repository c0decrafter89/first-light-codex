I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Units of time measured by the earth's orbit around the sun—frequently invoked as a ledger of accumulated loss ("the years are catching up with me") or scarcity ("I don't have the years left").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Years" in aging-anxious culture becomes an adversarial tally, a countdown toward depletion, training a dread relationship with the passage of time rather than a felt sense of accompanying seasonal cycles.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English gear, Proto-Indo-European yer- (year, season)—distantly related to Greek hora (hour, season, the right time). A year was originally a season's full cycle, a natural rhythm, not an adversarial countdown.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a natural, cyclical rhythm has been inverted by aging-anxious culture into a scarcity ledger, counting down toward depletion. The seasonal cycle became an adversary tallying loss.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: YEARS \rightarrow GEAR / SEASONAL-CYCLES. We restore the cyclical root: years are not a depleting countdown, but seasonal cycles—each one a complete rhythm, not a withdrawal from a finite account.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing years as seasonal cycles rather than an adversarial countdown relaxes the dread-bracing around aging, allowing the passage of time to be felt as accompanying rhythm, like seasons turning, rather than threat.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while picturing a full seasonal turn—"Years... seasonal-cycles"—feeling rhythm, not depletion.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "the years are running out on me" with "I am moving through seasonal cycles, each one its own complete rhythm."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My years are not a scarcity ledger counting down. They are seasonal cycles, each complete in itself, and I remain sovereign within their unfolding rhythm.
