I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To operate a vehicle; metaphorically, strong motivation or ambition ("she has drive")—often framed as relentless, forceful push with no room for ease.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Drive" as ambition trains an exhausting, forceful self-relationship—driving yourself implies constant pushing, as though motivation must always be generated through force rather than being allowed to flow.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English drifan (to drive, to force to move, to pursue)—related to "drift." The oldest sense carried both forceful pursuit and the gentler sense of drifting, being carried along—the word held both force and flow from its earliest use.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word originally holding both forceful pursuit and gentle drift has been narrowed by hustle-culture into pure, relentless force. The drift half of the word's nature was dropped, leaving only the exhausting push.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: DRIVE \rightarrow DRIFAN / FORCE-AND-DRIFT. We restore the dual nature: drive is not only forceful push, but also drift—sometimes propelling with force, sometimes simply being carried along by momentum already present.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing drive as holding both force and drift relaxes the exhausting, unilateral pushing that ambition-culture demands, allowing motivation to alternate naturally between active propulsion and restful, carried momentum.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while alternating a pushing gesture with an open, drifting one—"Drive... drifan, force-and-drift"—honoring both.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I must constantly force my own drive" with "my drive can also drift, carried by momentum I don't have to generate alone."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My drive is not only relentless force. It is drifan, force-and-drift together, and I am sovereign to let momentum carry me as often as I choose to push it.
