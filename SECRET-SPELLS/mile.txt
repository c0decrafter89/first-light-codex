I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A unit of linear distance (5,280 feet), used to quantify how far a body has traveled or must still travel.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Mile" abstracts the living experience of a journey into a cold, standardized number, training the mind to measure movement by imposed metric rather than felt distance. It converts the qualitative richness of a walk—terrain, breath, weather, company—into a flat quantitative marker to be logged and compared.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin mille passus, "a thousand paces"—the distance a Roman soldier's marching stride covered in one thousand steps. Beneath the abstract unit is a deeply embodied origin: the mile was once literally counted in footfalls, not imposed by external standard.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A measurement rooted in the rhythm of a marching body's own footsteps has been inverted by standardization into an externally fixed, impersonal unit, severed from any individual body's actual stride, breath, or pace. The felt thousand-paces became the abstract 5,280 feet.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: MILE \rightarrow MY-LE / THOUSAND-STEP-MINE. We restore the embodied pace: a mile is not an external decree but your own thousand steps, felt and counted in your own rhythm.
 * Physiological Translation: Reframing distance as self-generated footfall rhythm rather than imposed metric relaxes the anxious, clock-watching bracing of "how far/how fast" performance culture. The body settles into its own pace as sufficient and sovereign, rather than measured against an external standard.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word with a personal inflection—"My-le"—feeling the possessive "my" restore ownership of the distance to your own body's pace.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I only did a mile" (external-metric shame) with "I walked my thousand steps, at my own rhythm"—restoring felt-pace over imposed measurement.
 * The Sovereign Realization: A mile is not handed down by an external ruler. It is my own thousand paces, sovereign and sufficient exactly as I walk them.
