I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Correct, just, or morally fitting; also, a direction, or an entitlement ("human rights")—a word carrying enormous moral and legal weight across very different contexts.
 * The Acoustic Trap: Because "right" means both "correct" and "entitled," being right and being entitled become unconsciously fused—training a binary, high-stakes relationship to correctness, as though being wrong forfeits legitimacy itself.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English riht, Proto-Indo-European reg- (to move in a straight line, to direct, to rule)—the same root as "rule," "regular," and "direct" (a striking echo already explored under "rule"). To be right was originally to be aligned, straight, true to a direction.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning simple alignment or straightness has been inflated by moral-and-legal culture into an absolute, binary verdict with high stakes attached. Being "off" from straight became a moral failing rather than a correctable alignment.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: RIGHT \rightarrow REG / RE-ALIGN. We restore the directional root: being right is not a permanent, binary verdict, but a matter of alignment—correctable, adjustable, never a fixed moral sentence.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "right" as alignment rather than binary moral verdict relaxes the defensive bracing that fear of being wrong produces, allowing correction to be felt as simple re-alignment rather than a threat to one's legitimacy.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while straightening your own posture—"Right... reg, re-align"—feeling adjustment, not verdict.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I was wrong, therefore I am bad" with "I was simply out of alignment, and I can re-align."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My rightness is not a fixed, binary verdict on my worth. It is ongoing alignment, reg, and I remain sovereign in continuously re-aligning as I go.
