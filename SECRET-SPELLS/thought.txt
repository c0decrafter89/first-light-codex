I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The past tense of "think," and the noun for an idea, mental process, or cognitive product—the currency of the mind in a culture that equates identity with mentation.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Thought" lands with a hard, final -t, sealing a mental event as a completed, solid object—something you "have," "own," or are chained to ("I can't stop thinking this thought"). It trains the mind to reify passing mental weather into fixed possessions, the seed of every looping rumination.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English þoht, past participle of þencan (to think), from Proto-Germanic thankjaną, related to Proto-Indo-European teng- (to feel, to know). Buried beneath cognition is feeling—thought was never meant to be purely mental machinery; it was born entangled with somatic sensing.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: The Cartesian, mechanistic culture severed thought from feeling, elevating "thought" into a disembodied, hyper-valued currency (the thinking machine) while demoting the felt-sense roots it grew from. This inversion traps consciousness in the head, cut off from the body's original knowing.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: THOUGHT \rightarrow THROUGH-T / FELT-THOUGHT. We reunite cognition with sensation: a thought is not a solid object lodged in the skull, but something that moves through the whole body—a through-current, not a thing possessed.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing thought as a felt current rather than a fixed possession releases the cranial bracing and shallow breath of obsessive rumination. The nervous system stops defending a mental object and instead lets sensation move through, restoring full-body cognitive flow.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word softened into motion—"Th-rough-t"—feeling it pass through the chest and belly rather than lodging behind the eyes.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: When a thought loops, silently reframe: "This is a current passing through me, not an object I must carry."
 * The Sovereign Realization: I do not own my thoughts as fixed possessions weighing me down. I am the spacious field through which felt-thought moves, free to arise and free to pass.
