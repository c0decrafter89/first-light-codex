I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A person who trains and directs athletes or individuals toward improvement—in self-help culture, increasingly a credentialed authority figure dispensing corrective instruction.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Coach" has drifted toward implying hierarchy—someone positioned above you, correcting your deficiencies. This obscures the word's origin as a vehicle that simply carries people forward together, not a figure of corrective authority standing apart.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From French coche (a large carriage), ultimately from Hungarian kocsi, named after Kocs, the town where such carriages were first built. A coach was, quite literally, a vehicle that carries passengers forward—the metaphorical extension to "one who guides your progress" is about transport, not hierarchy.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a shared vehicle carrying people together has been inverted by credentialing culture into a figure of corrective, hierarchical authority. The carriage carrying everyone forward became the instructor positioned above.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: COACH \rightarrow KOCSI / SHARED-VEHICLE. We restore the carriage root: a coach is not an authority correcting your deficiencies, but a shared vehicle, carrying you and itself forward together.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing coaching as shared transport rather than hierarchical correction relaxes the defensive bracing that "being coached" (judged, corrected) can trigger, restoring a felt sense of being carried alongside rather than overseen from above.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word evenly, as a vehicle in motion—"Coach... carriage"—feeling forward movement, not hierarchy.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "my coach is correcting my flaws" with "we are both in this shared vehicle, moving forward together."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My coach is not an authority standing above me, cataloguing deficiencies. They are a shared vehicle, and I remain a sovereign passenger, carried forward, not corrected from on high.
