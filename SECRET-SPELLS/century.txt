I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A period of one hundred years, used to segment history into tidy, numbered administrative blocks (the 19th century, the 21st century).
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Century" imposes an artificial, arithmetic grid onto the organic flow of lived time. It trains the mind to experience history as a filing cabinet of hundred-year drawers rather than an unbroken, living continuum—reinforcing the industrial habit of chopping the seamless into countable, manageable units.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin centuria (a group of one hundred), from centum (hundred). In ancient Rome, a "centuria" was originally a military unit of soldiers and, separately, a voting division of citizens—a word born from grouping people, not measuring years.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word once meaning a living assembly of a hundred people has been abstracted into a pure numerical container for time, severing the human, communal root from the concept. Time became a census-ledger entry rather than a gathering of lived generations.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: CENTURY \rightarrow SENT-URE-Y / CENTER-Y. We restore the buried gathering: a century is not a numbered drawer but a "sent-ure"—a hundred lives sent forward together, centered around shared living memory.
 * Physiological Translation: Reframing time as gathered lives rather than counted units relaxes the mind's habitual grip on linear, ledger-time anxiety. The body settles into ancestral, cyclical time-sense—a felt continuity with those who came before and those still arriving.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word slowly, feeling the "cent" as a hundred hands joining—"Cen-tu-ry"—a gathering, not a grid line.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "this century" (an abstract ledger entry) with "this gathering of lives"—restoring the human, communal texture to historical time.
 * The Sovereign Realization: I do not live inside a numbered administrative drawer of time. I am one life within a great gathering, centuries deep, unbroken and alive.
