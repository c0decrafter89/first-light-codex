I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The speed of movement or progress; also, a single step—often used to demand conformity to an external tempo ("keep pace," "pacesetter").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Pace" in competitive and workplace language trains the body to measure itself against someone else's rhythm, converting a word for one's own natural stepping-rhythm into an externally imposed demand for synchronization.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin passus (a step), the same root as "pass" and "passage." A pace was originally simply the measure of one's own single step—an intimate, personal unit, not a benchmark set by someone else.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for one's own personal step-measure has been inverted by competitive culture into an external standard to be matched or exceeded. The intimate unit of self-measurement became a comparative demand.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: PACE \rightarrow PASSUS / OWN-STEP. We restore the personal root: your pace is not an external benchmark to match, but your own step—measured by your own body, in your own rhythm.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing pace as one's own personal step-measure rather than an externally imposed demand relaxes the comparative anxiety of "keeping pace," allowing the body to settle into its own sustainable, sovereign rhythm.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while taking one deliberate step of your own—"Pace... own-step"—feeling your rhythm, not someone else's.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I need to keep pace with everyone else" with "my pace is my own step, measured in my own body's rhythm."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My pace is not a benchmark set by others. It is my own step, sovereignly measured, and no external rhythm can rightfully claim it.
