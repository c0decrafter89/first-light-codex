I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A single movement of drawing something across a surface; medically, a sudden loss of brain function from blocked or burst blood flow.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The collision between a gentle, gliding motion and a catastrophic medical emergency embeds a strange dual charge into the word—an unconscious flinch, as though any prolonged, repeated stroking motion secretly borders on danger. The tender gesture and the crisis share one breath.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English strac (a stroke, a blow), related to "strike"—both senses, gentle and catastrophic, share the same ancestral root of a single, deliberate motion landing somewhere. The difference was always in intention and force, not in the word itself.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A single root meaning "one deliberate motion" split into opposite poles—violence and tenderness—yet the medical sense has come to overshadow the gentle one, casting a faint shadow of danger over even the softest, most caring gesture.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: STROKE \rightarrow SOFT-STRIKE / ONE-DELIBERATE-PASS. We separate the poles: a tender stroke is a soft, deliberate pass, sovereign and gentle by intention, wholly distinct from its catastrophic homonym.
 * Physiological Translation: Naming the intention clearly—soft, deliberate, chosen—releases the unconscious flinch the medical association can trigger, allowing the skin and nervous system to receive gentle, repeated contact as pure regulation and comfort, not shadowed by crisis.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word slowly and softly—"Strooo-ke... soft pass"—matching the pace of an actual gentle stroke.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace any unconscious flinch at the word with "this is a soft, deliberate pass, chosen and safe."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My gentle stroke is not shadowed by crisis. It is my own soft, deliberate pass—chosen tenderness, plainly and safely itself.
