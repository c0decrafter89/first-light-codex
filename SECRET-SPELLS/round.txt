I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Shaped like a circle or sphere; also, a single segment of a competition (a round of boxing, a round of golf)—dividing continuous time into discrete, countable units.
 * The Acoustic Trap: Segmenting experience into "rounds" trains linear, countdown thinking even onto an inherently circular word—every round becomes one fewer remaining, a depletion-count layered atop a shape that, by its nature, has no beginning or end.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin rotundus (wheel-shaped), from rota (wheel)—the same root as "rotate" and "roll." A round is, at its deepest root, simply the shape of a wheel: complete, continuous, without a true start or finish.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for a complete, continuous, wheel-like shape has been inverted by competition-structure into a countdown of discrete, depleting segments. The seamless circle became a ticking scoreboard.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: ROUND \rightarrow ROTUNDUS / WHEEL-WHOLE. We restore the wheel-root: a round is not a depleting countdown segment, but a complete, wheel-like whole—continuous, without true beginning or end.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing each round as a complete wheel rather than a depleting fragment relaxes the countdown-anxiety of competitive segmenting, allowing each round to be felt as whole and sufficient in itself, not merely "one less remaining."
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while tracing a full circle with your hand—"Round... wheel-whole"—feeling completeness, not depletion.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "one round down, fewer left" with "this round is a complete wheel, whole in itself."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My round is not a depleting fragment counted down to zero. It is a complete, wheel-like whole, sovereign and sufficient exactly as it is.
