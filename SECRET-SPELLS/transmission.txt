I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The mechanical system that transfers an engine's power to the wheels; also, the act of sending information or a disease from one party to another.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The disease-sense ("viral transmission") dominates public health discourse, training an unconscious association between the word and contagion—every "transmission," mechanical or informational, carries a faint whiff of infectious danger.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin transmittere, trans- (across) + mittere (to send)—the same root as "mission" and "transmit." A transmission is, at root, simply a sending-across—a neutral act of conveyance, carrying power, information, or anything else from one point to another.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A neutral word for conveyance across distance has been inverted by epidemiological-crisis framing into an automatic association with contagion and danger. Simple sending-across became suspect transfer.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: TRANSMISSION \rightarrow TRANS-MITTERE / SENDING-ACROSS. We restore the neutral root: a transmission is simply a sending-across—power, knowledge, or signal, conveyed from one point to another, not inherently a contagion.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "transmission" as neutral conveyance rather than automatic contagion relaxes the unconscious suspicion the word can trigger, allowing power, knowledge, or energy to be sent and received as straightforward exchange.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while making a sending gesture with your hand, outward across space—"Transmission... trans-mittere, sending-across"—neutral conveyance, not contagion.
