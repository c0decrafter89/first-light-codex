I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A continuously bending line with no straight sections; in driving idiom, often framed as a hazard requiring reduced speed and caution, a deviation from the "proper" straight path.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Curve" in road-safety framing is treated as an obstacle to the ideal of the straight line—training the sense that deviation from directness is inherently risky, rather than simply a different, equally valid shape of path.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin curvus (bent, curved, arched)—the same root as "curvature." The word itself carries no inherent judgment of hazard; it simply names a shape, one found throughout the natural world in rivers, coastlines, and growing vines.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A neutral word for a natural, bending shape has been inverted by straight-line-idealizing road-safety culture into a marker of caution and risk. Nature's preferred shape became a hazard sign.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: CURVE \rightarrow CURVUS / NATURAL-BEND. We restore the natural root: a curve is not an inherent hazard, but a natural bend—the same shape rivers, vines, and coastlines have always favored over rigid straightness.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing curves as natural, graceful bends rather than inherent hazards invites the body to move through them with the same flowing ease a river follows its own bend, rather than rigid, anxious caution.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while tracing a flowing, bending line with your hand—"Curve... curvus, natural-bend"—ease, not hazard.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "a curve means danger, deviation from the proper path" with "a curve is simply nature's own preferred, flowing shape."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My curve is not an inherent hazard deviating from a proper straight line. It is curvus, a natural bend, as graceful and legitimate as any river's course.
