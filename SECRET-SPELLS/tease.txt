I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: To provoke playfully, or to withhold something desired while gesturing toward it—in interpersonal dynamics, sometimes weaponized as a form of mockery or manipulative withholding.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Tease" carries an ambiguous charge—it can mean gentle play or cruel manipulation, and the word rarely distinguishes which. This ambiguity trains uncertainty into the body: is this safe delight, or is something being withheld unkindly? The nervous system often can't settle either way.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English tæsan (to pluck apart, to comb or card wool)—a craftsperson's term for separating and loosening fibers before spinning. Teasing was originally patient, skillful loosening work, not provocation or withholding at all.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the careful, skillful loosening of fiber has been inverted by interpersonal dynamics into a form of psychological push-pull. The craftsperson's patient hands became a manipulative gesture of approach-and-withdrawal.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: TEASE \rightarrow TÆS-CARD / LOOSEN-GENTLY. We restore the craft-root: healthy teasing is not manipulative withholding, but a gentle, skillful loosening—patient hands working knots free, never closing them tighter.
 * Physiological Translation: Distinguishing skillful, consensual loosening from manipulative withholding relaxes the whiplash-bracing (approach then flinch) that ambiguous teasing can trigger, restoring a felt sense of safety when play is genuinely, consensually playful.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word slowly—"Teee-ase... loosen"—feeling for whether the gesture actually loosens or actually tightens.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace uncertain tolerance of teasing with a clear check: "is this loosening me gently, or closing a knot tighter?"—naming the difference plainly.
 * The Sovereign Realization: True teasing loosens, never tightens. I am sovereign enough to name the difference and welcome only the gentle, skillful kind.
