I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Sustained physical or mental effort directed toward a purpose—in capitalist culture, almost entirely synonymous with paid employment, severing effort from its intrinsic meaning outside of wages.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Work" has been colonized by labor-economics so thoroughly that unpaid effort (caregiving, art, rest-as-recovery) struggles to register as legitimate "work" at all, training a worth-hierarchy where only compensated effort counts.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English weorc, Proto-Indo-European werg- (to do, to act)—the same ancient root behind "organ," "energy," and "ergonomic." Work, at its deepest root, is simply the activity of a living organ doing what it is organized to do.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the organic activity of any living system doing its function has been inverted by wage-labor economics into a narrow, compensated category. The heart's work, the mind's work, and the hands' uncompensated work were all quietly excluded.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: WORK \rightarrow WERG / ORGAN-ACTING. We restore the organic root: work is simply any living organ doing what it is organized to do—not confined to what a paycheck recognizes.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing all purposeful effort as legitimate organ-acting rather than only wage-labor relaxes the worth-anxiety around unpaid activity, allowing caregiving, creating, and resting-to-recover to be felt as genuine work.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling an actual organ's rhythm (heartbeat, breath)—"Work... werg, organ-acting"—restoring its organic root.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "this doesn't count as real work because it isn't paid" with "this is my organ, organism, and purpose, legitimately at work."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My work is not confined to what a wage recognizes. It is my own living organ-acting, and it is sovereign and legitimate whether or not it is ever compensated.
