I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The playing field in cricket or soccer, or the act of throwing a ball; also, a sales presentation, or the level of a musical tone.
 * The Acoustic Trap: The sales-pitch sense has colonized the word's emotional register—"pitch" now implies persuasion, performance, and the anxiety of being evaluated, bleeding into even its sporting and musical senses as a subtle undertone of being "sold."
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English piccean (to thrust, to fix firmly in place)—the oldest sense is about setting something solidly, as in "pitching a tent." The throwing sense derives from this fixing-motion: hurling something toward a fixed point.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word about solid, deliberate fixing-in-place has been extended by commerce into persuasive performance—the firm plant of a tent stake became the anxious thrust of a sales presentation.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: PITCH \rightarrow PICCEAN / FIRM-PLANT. We restore the fixing root: a pitch is not an anxious performance for approval, but a firm, deliberate plant—setting something solidly toward its intended place.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "pitching" as firm, grounded placement rather than anxious persuasion relaxes the performance-bracing of sales-pitch anxiety, allowing delivery (of a ball, an idea, a tone) to come from settled confidence rather than desperate selling.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word with a grounded, confident landing—"Pitch... firm-plant"—feeling solidity, not sales-anxiety.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I need to sell this pitch" with "I am firmly planting this toward its intended place."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My pitch is not an anxious performance for approval. It is a firm, deliberate plant—solid, grounded, and sovereign in its own placement.
