I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The system or act of starting combustion in an engine—metaphorically, the spark that begins any intense process or passion, often treated with wariness as something volatile or dangerous.
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Ignition" carries an undertone of danger and combustibility—something to be carefully controlled lest it get out of hand, training a wariness around one's own sparks of passion or inspiration as potentially hazardous rather than simply generative.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Latin ignis (fire)—the same root as "igneous." To ignite is, at its simplest, "to fire"—to bring forth the elemental, generative force that has powered human civilization since its earliest flickering spark.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the elemental, life-giving gift of fire has been inverted by safety-conscious industrial culture into a hazard to be cautiously contained. The ancient gift of flame became primarily a danger to be managed.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: IGNITION \rightarrow IGNIS / ELEMENTAL-FIRE. We restore the elemental root: ignition is not primarily a hazard, but elemental fire—the generative, life-giving force humanity has always depended on to create warmth, light, and motion.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing ignition as elemental, generative fire rather than primarily a hazard invites the body to welcome its own sparks of passion and inspiration as life-giving, not dangerous, restoring warmth and creative combustion as trustworthy forces.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while feeling a spark of genuine enthusiasm rise in your chest—"Ignition... ignis, elemental-fire"—welcoming it, not bracing against it.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "this spark of passion is dangerous, I need to control it" with "this is ignis, elemental fire, generative and life-giving."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My ignition is not primarily a hazard to contain. It is ignis, elemental fire, and I am sovereign enough to welcome my own generative sparks.
