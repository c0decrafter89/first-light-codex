I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: The cardinal number following three—a purely quantitative marker used in counting, measurement, and accounting.
 * The Acoustic Trap: As a homophone of "for," "four" quietly smuggles purpose and exchange into pure arithmetic—the number that stands "for" a quantity becomes entangled with obligation and transaction. Numbers in industrial culture are rarely neutral; they are always counted "for" something—production, currency, allotment—training the mind to see quantity as inherently tied to utility and account-keeping.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English fēower, Proto-Germanic fedwōr, ultimately Proto-Indo-European kwetwer- (four)—a root of deep antiquity, present across nearly the entire Indo-European family, often associated in ancient cosmology with the four cardinal directions and four elements: a number of wholeness and orientation, not mere tally.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A number once symbolizing cosmic wholeness—the four directions, the four elements, the four seasons—has been inverted by accounting culture into a flat unit stripped of its orienting, sacred symmetry. Wholeness became mere tally-mark.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: FOUR \rightarrow FOUR-DIRECTIONS / WHOLE-QUARTER. We restore the cosmological root: four is not a bare digit but the number of complete orientation—north, south, east, west; earth, air, fire, water—a symbol of wholeness, not accounting.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "four" as an orienting, cosmological number rather than a bare tally invites the body to feel its own four-limbed symmetry as balanced wholeness. The nervous system settles into the felt sense of complete, stable orientation rather than fragmented counting.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while turning slowly to face each cardinal direction in turn—"Fooour"—physically embodying the wholeness the number once represented.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "just a number" thinking around four with "the number of complete orientation"—restoring cosmological wholeness over bare tally.
