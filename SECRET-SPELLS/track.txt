I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: A path, course, or set of rails for movement; metaphorically, to monitor or follow closely ("track your progress")—the vocabulary of surveillance and self-quantification.
 * The Acoustic Trap: In the age of fitness apps and productivity dashboards, "track" has become a surveillance word—every metric tracked, every step logged—training a relationship to one's own movement as data to be monitored rather than lived.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old French trac (a trace, the mark left by passing animals), likely related to a Germanic root meaning "to drag" or "to pull." A track was originally the honest, physical trace left behind by movement—not a number on a dashboard.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for the honest physical trace left by living movement has been inverted by quantification culture into surveillance data. The footprint in the earth became a number in an app.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: TRACK \rightarrow TRAC / LIVING-TRACE. We restore the footprint root: a track is not surveillance data, but the honest, physical trace a living body leaves as it moves through the world.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing a track as a felt, physical trace rather than monitored data relaxes the self-surveillance anxiety that constant metric-tracking produces, restoring attention to the lived sensation of movement itself.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word while imagining a footprint left in soft earth—"Tra-ck... trace"—feeling the physical mark, not the data point.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "am I tracking my metrics correctly" with "what honest trace is my living movement leaving right now?"
 * The Sovereign Realization: My track is not surveillance data to be optimized. It is my own honest, living trace, left simply because I moved through the world at all.
