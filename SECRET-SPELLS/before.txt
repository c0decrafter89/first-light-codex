I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Earlier in time or position—often used to measure present inadequacy against a nostalgic or idealized past ("better off before," "I was happier before").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Before" in nostalgic framing becomes a comparison-trap, training the mind to measure the present against an idealized, often distorted prior state, implicitly devaluing now in favor of a frozen, unreturnable then.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English beforan, be- (by) + foran (in front)—literally "in front of," a word of simple spatial and temporal positioning, originally carrying no built-in comparative judgment.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word for neutral positioning ("in front of") has been inverted by nostalgic comparison-culture into a tool for devaluing the present. Simple positioning became a wistful, unfavorable comparison.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: BEFORE \rightarrow BE-FORAN / IN-FRONT-OF. We restore the neutral root: before is simply "in front of" in sequence—not an automatic verdict that the present falls short of it.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "before" as neutral positioning rather than nostalgic comparison relaxes the wistful bracing of "it was better before," allowing the present to be met on its own terms rather than measured against an idealized prior frame.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word plainly, as simple sequence—"Before... in-front-of"—without nostalgic weight.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "it was better before" with "before is simply what came in front of now; now stands on its own."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My "before" is not a golden standard devaluing my present. It is simply what came in front, and my now remains sovereign and whole on its own terms.
