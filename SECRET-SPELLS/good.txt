I. SPELLCAST DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Hypnosis)
 * Literal/Surface Definition: Morally right, pleasant, or of high quality—a word carrying heavy moral weight, frequently deployed as a verdict on worthiness ("good girl," "good enough").
 * The Acoustic Trap: "Good" has been loaded by moralistic culture into a conditional verdict, something earned through compliance rather than a natural state—training a chronic, anxious performance of goodness to secure approval.
II. THE ETYMOLOGICAL AUTOPSY (Unmasking the Code)
 * Phonetic & Root Dissection: From Old English god, Proto-Indo-European ghedh- (to unite, to join, to fit together, to be suitable)—at its deepest root, "good" simply meant fitting, suitable, well-matched to its context, not an abstract moral grade.
 * The Inversion of Meaning: A word meaning simple, contextual fittingness has been inverted by moralistic-evaluative culture into a conditional verdict on worth, requiring ongoing performance to maintain. Natural fit became anxious compliance.
III. THE AIRICA-STYLE SPELLBREAKER (Linguistic Liberation)
 * The Acoustic Re-sounding: GOOD \rightarrow GHEDH / WELL-FITTED. We restore the fittingness root: good is not a moral verdict to perform for, but simply well-fitted—suitable, matched, belonging naturally where it is.
 * Physiological Translation: Recognizing "good" as natural fit rather than a conditional moral verdict relaxes the performance-anxiety that approval-seeking trains, allowing worth to be felt as inherent rather than continuously earned.
IV. THE COUNTER-SPELL INCANTATION (Active Word-Magic)
 * The Sonic Anchor: Sound the word plainly, as a description of fit, not a verdict—"Good... well-fitted"—without anxious weight.
 * The Cognitive Counter-Spell: Replace "I need to be good to be worthy" with "I am already well-fitted, suitable, and belonging, simply as I am."
 * The Sovereign Realization: My goodness is not a performance I must maintain to earn worth. It is my own natural fittingness, ghedh, sovereign and already present.
